## The game
You start in the hub cave. Outside, five bases sit in a row in front of the cave mouth (the player owns the middle one, `BASES.home`); each has a mine cart on its own rails out of its station, but the four side tracks curve in and join the home track on the valley floor (`TRACK.merge`, `mergeK` / `laneX` in cartPhysics.js), so from the canyon ramp and the broken bridge to Golden Station there is one shared line. Hold E on the cart for 1 s (or press LAUNCH) to ride: W / GAS accelerates while fuel lasts, S / BRAKE brakes, Space / Shift / BOOST fires a booster. Run cash = `floor(distance / Distance Per Currency) * Currency * Currency Multiplier * multipliers` (`state.runPayout`; the Cart Upgrades are bought at the base stall, `UPGRADES`: `kind: 'cart'` rows Engine Power / Fuel Tank / Aerodynamics feed `cartStats` (thrust, tank, rolling + air drag), `kind: 'cash'` rows Distance Per Currency / Currency Multiplier / Currency feed the payout; multipliers = extra carts, Golden Carts, rebirths, passes, wheel boost, event), banked when the cart stops, on RETURN, or after a crash (REVIVE costs Cash and puts you back at `CART.safeS` with a full tank). Every extra cart is a fuel wagon: +`CART.wagonFuel` tank each, thrust / (1 + n * `CART.wagonMass`). The goal: upgrade the cart (the Shop stall in the cave, restocks every 5 min; Cart Upgrades at the base stall; Starter Pack and OP V8 display pads at the base) until it is fast enough at the lip to clear the gap. Past the canyon the line runs on through the Great Arch (`TRACK.archS`) into the new areas (`ZONES`, built by `zones.js`), ending at Golden Station (`TRACK.length` = 3230 m): Bumpy Meadows (airtime humps), Crystal River (bridge over the water), Waterfall Gorge (the Sky Trestle climbs 34 m over a lake, then plunges through the waterfall), Glow Mine (one bore through the mountain) and Golden Sands (desert humps, the station's brakes stop the cart at the buffers and end the run). Each area shows a title card on entry; the first run that gets past the player's best into it pays its `bonus`. The line in order: Sunny Farm (barn, silos, windmill, cows, a waving farmer), the Broken Canyon, Whispering Forest (a gentle bend, the deer + moose crossing, a bear if you wait too long), Switchback Hills (three tight bends: brake and lean), the Mountain Pass (chain lift to a 30 m summit, a dive through the Mountain Tunnel), Bumpy Meadows, Woolly Pasture (sheep crossing), Crystal River (the home track's drawbridge lifts as you come), the rockslide, Waterfall Gorge (crosswind gusts on the Sky Trestle), Glow Mine, Golden Sands (sandstorm + the Gem Run junction) and Golden Station (brake on the gold line for PERFECT STOP).

Ride mechanics (route update): bends shift every track sideways by `trackX(s)` (`TRACK.bends`); in a bend the cart is pushed out by v^2 x curvature and the rider leans with A / D (touch: the round lean buttons): `c.tip` = (push / `LEAN.accel` - lean) / wheel `grip`; past `LEAN.green` the cart rides on two wheels (sparks, screech, PERFECT LEAN / DRIFT KING bonuses), past 1 for `LEAN.derailTime` it derails (crash 'derail'). `stepCart` without `input.lean` (headless sims, devtests/sim.js) leans perfectly and brakes for bends itself. The chain lift (`TRACK.lift`) hauls slow carts up at a walk (no fuel needed). `HAZARDS` block the home track at `at` for a window once triggered (trigger distance grows with v^2 so there is always room to brake); arrive blocked under `bump` m/s = a bump, faster = a crash ('herd', 'rocks', 'splash'); stopping and waiting pays a patience bonus; hazards trigger one at a time in order. REVIVE puts the cart at the last `CHECKPOINTS` entry behind the crash (the canyon still uses `CART.safeS`). Pickups and skill bonuses (`PICKUPS`) are worth metres of run pay (`state.bonusCash`), so they scale with the Cart Upgrades. Rain (Thunderstorm) cuts grip (`LEAN.rainGrip`). All prices are Cash (root CLAUDE.md rule: nothing is sold for premium currency).

Balance: `cartPhysics.js` is pure, so gear combos can be simulated in Node (import config.js + cartPhysics.js and step `stepCart`). The canyon ramp starts at 240 m: starting gear tops out ~260 m (the foot of the ramp), Engine + Small Fuel + Rubber ~320 m (up the ramp, rolls back); the cheapest clear is V12 + Small Fuel + Firework (~$1.2k), and the Starter Pack (V12, Big Fuel, Sport, Firework) clears it. The top shop gear is rebirth-locked (Racing Wheels 1, Twin Nitro 2, Hover Pads 3, Afterburner 4, Hamburger / Fries 5). A greedy-buyer sim (cash per minute, ~25 s overhead per run) makes the first jump ~17 min, Bumpy Meadows ~27 min, rebirth 1 ~1 h, Golden Station ~2.3 h after two rebirths. Cart physics fix to keep: the far deck's "rolled back to the broken end" guard only applies to a cart coming from the far side (`before >= gapEnd`); otherwise any cart reaching the near lip would be teleported across. Past the far deck the profile is `TRACK.hills` (key points joined by smoothsteps); over a crest a fast cart leaves the rails (`hop` / `touchdown` events, rail drag in the air so hops are no speed trick); out of fuel and rolling back `CART.rollbackEnd` behind the best ends the run. Sim script pattern: step `stepCart` with full throttle per gear set and log `zoneAt(c.s)`. Engines show `speed` (accel = speed / 10).

Three.js (vanilla, no React) + Vite + Rapier (`@dimforge/rapier3d-compat`, wasm inlined; `main.js` awaits its init at top level, hence `build.target: 'es2022'`). Everything is procedural (canvas textures); the only imported model is the Bloxity base rig `public/avatars/player.glb`. Font: Fredoka (loaded in `index.html`).

- `npm run dev` / `npm run build`. `VITE_DEV_MODE=true` (see `.env.example`) skips the Bloxity SDK/CDN. Deploy: `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` uploads `dist/` to Bloxity Hosting (main = prod, dev = dev channel). The game slug is the repo/folder name lowercased (`build-a-cart`); repo var/secret `LEGION_GAME_ID` is only an optional override; secret `LEGION_DEPLOY_TOKEN` is required.
- All tunables are in `src/config.js` (the rest of `src/` holds no theme values): identity, `SPAWN`, `MODES`, `PHYSICS`, the look (`RENDER`, `FOG`, `TIME_OF_DAY`, `LIGHT`, `GRAPHICS_PRESETS`, `BLOOM`, `NEON`), `TOUCH`, `CAMERA`, `SETTINGS`, the rig (`RIG*`, `PROPORTIONS`, `ANIM`), and the game: `VALLEY`, `CAVE`, `BASES`, `TRACK` (the shared rail height profile and the new areas' landmarks: river, gorge, falls, mine, station), `ZONES` / `zoneAt`, `CART` (ride physics), `RARITY` / `GEAR` / `DEFAULT_GEAR` / `INDEX_TABS` / `BUNDLES`, `ECONOMY`, `UPGRADES`, `REBIRTH`, `RESTOCK` / `INSTANT_MARKUP`, `PASSES`, `WHEEL`, `EVENTS`, `QUESTS` / `QUEST_STATS` / `QUEST_LEVELS` / `DICE`, `SAVE_SLOTS`, `SERVER_URL` / `NET`, `AUDIO`. The backend's quest pools (`QUEST_POOLS` in its constants.ts) must list the same quest ids as `QUESTS`.

## Multiplayer (5 players per room) and Bloxity features
- **Room**: one `world` room, `maxClients` 5 (backend `ROOM_MAX_CLIENTS`; the deploy workflow sends it as `roomSize`, hosted party play seats a party of up to `MULTI.maxPlayers` in one room). Every client plays on the HOME (middle) base; the other players take the four remaining bases (`MULTI.basePrefs` = 1, 3, 0, 4), assigned client-side on first sight and kept until they leave (the bases are not shared state, only the room roster is).
- **Sending** (`presence.js`, only when changed + a 1 s heartbeat): `pose` on foot (`x,y,z` world coords, `yaw`, `speed`, `grounded`; `NET.poseEvery`), `cart` while riding (`ride.presence()`: `ride` riding/crashed/ending/foot, `crash` kind, `s`, `cy`, `pitch`, `roll`, `lx` lane, `v`, `throttle`, `braking`, `boosting`, `air`, `fuelOk`, `tip`; `NET.cartEvery`), `loadout` (equipped gear ids + `wagons` + `golden`), `setAvatar` (`avatar` = JSON `{ equipped, proportions }` ids only + `outfit` = Bloxity outfit key), `emote` (portal emote id, `''` stops). Servers bound everything again (backend `presence.ts`).
- **Drawing** (`remote.js`, `createRemotePlayers`, diffed against `net.players` every frame): per remote a parked cart on its base with its gear + trailing carts and the avatar walking (the walk is shifted by the base offset outside, true spot in the hub cave, shown there only to a viewer in the cave); while riding the cart sits on the shared line (`laneX(x0, HOME, s) + lx`, dead-reckoned from `s` / `v`), the avatar in the seat, wheels spin, trailing carts follow a breadcrumb trail, exhaust / flames / booster fire / brake + wheel sparks / dust within `MULTI.smokeRange`, crash = explosion and the cart is gone, revive = confetti. Name tags, chat bubbles, emotes (`avatar/emotes.js`) and looks (`avatar/look.js`: Bloxity character kit by outfit key, else the manual CDN path, else the base rig). `tracks.setBaseOwner(i, { name, status })` swaps the base's OPEN BASE sign + decorative cart for the player's name sign.
- **Bloxity SDK features in `bloxity.js`** (all never-throw wrappers; the game works without the SDK): init + loading steps + `gameplayStart/End`, auth (`onUserChanged`, user/guest) and the game token (`getToken`, sent as join option `token`: the server verifies it and uses only the id Bloxity returns), settings (`graphics_quality`, `camera_sensitivity`, `show_fps`, `fullscreen`, `master_volume`), avatar + proportions events and customizer, `respawn_request`, reserved UI areas, invite link, matchmaker (`net.resolveEndpoint`, `?roomId=` launch rooms, `game.updateRoom`), **party play** (`party.enable({ maxPartySize: 5 })`, `getLaunch` / `joined` / `failed`), **friend toasts** (`game.playerJoined` / `playerInRoom`), **emotes** (`registerFeature('emotes')`, `play_emote` / `stop_emote`, replicated through the room), **chat bubbles** (`chat.onMessage` + `attachBubble`, local + remote), **ads** (`onBreakStart/End` freeze + mute the game, `declareScreens`, `screen('menu')` while a window is open and `'results'` on the crash screen, rewarded ads in `ads.js`: FREE REVIVE after a crash and x2 CASH for 10 min; the reward is granted only on `completed` AND the server confirming the token via `adReward`), **death screen** (`deathScreen.show()` on a crash: its countdown gates REVIVE / RESTART, never the game's own timer), **leaderboards** (`leaderboard.define` for `LEADERBOARDS`, which must match the backend `PLATFORM_BOARDS`; the server reports signed-in players' scores, the client only `submit`s the best distance when no server is reachable), **outfit keys** for other players' looks. **Gems are never used** (root CLAUDE.md): rewarded ads only give small perks, everything is bought with Cash.

## Browser tests (`src/devtests/`, dev server only)
Open the dev server with `?tests` (panel, click Run all) or `?tests=run` (starts at once; `&suite=<name part>`, `&speed=watch` for real time). `window.__tests.run('<filter>')` does the same from the console and resolves with the report. The tests play like a person: real key events (walk with W, hold E at prompts), real clicks on the menu buttons, real rides stepped frame by frame through `__game.frame` (the rAF loop is paused for the run and the player's own save is backed up and put back, saving is paused meanwhile via `net.setSavePaused`). Suites: `world`, `upgrades` (every level of every Cart Upgrade through the window), `shop` (every Shop item, carrying parts, sell, backpack, index, bundles, passes, all extra carts), `ride` (starter run, touch controls, brake, booster, crash / revive / restart, canyon clear, the whole line to Golden Station, weather events, simulator parity), `meta` (rebirth, gift, wheel, quests, save slots, save round trip) and `campaign` (a bot plays from a new save to Golden Station; `sim.js` is a headless copy of the ride + banking). Nothing is hard-coded: prices, counts and gear come from `config.js`, so a balance change should not break them; a test that fails after a game change usually means the game changed behaviour. Quests are muted in test setup (`h.muteQuests`) because their cash rewards pop up mid-test; the Quests test turns them on. Keep new features covered here: add a test to the matching suite.
Rides are driven by a careful player model, `devtests/pilot.js`, used by both `drive.js` (real rides: it leans with A / D, brakes for every bend ahead on a speed profile, stops short of a blocked hazard line and waits, caps the speed at the canyon lip to just over what clears it, no gas in the air, fires boosters only on the ramp and only if the cart would not clear without one) and `sim.js` (the headless ride follows the same rules and also collects the fuel cans / boost pads and waits out the hazards), so a real ride and its simulation stay comparable. Cash during a ride is split: `run.bonus` = coins, gems, skill bonuses, jump and new-area bonuses that arrive mid-ride; `run.banked` = the distance money banked when the run ends (tests check `banked` exactly and the rest as "at least the one-off bonuses"). Dev hooks the tests use: `ride.debug`, `ride.encounters.hazards`, `net.setSavePaused`. When ride mechanics change (new hazards, bends, pickups), update `pilot.js` and `sim.js` first.

## Mobile & responsive (required for every change)
The game must play on phones and tablets (portrait and landscape) as well as desktop. Treat a feature as unfinished until it works on touch and on a small screen.
- **Input**: every action needs a touch path, not just a key. Use Pointer Events (`pointerdown/move/up`, check `e.pointerType`), never mouse-only events. Layout (implemented in `src/touch.js`): left-thumb virtual joystick -> `inputState.move`, right-side drag -> `inputState.look`, on-screen buttons for jump/sprint/mode/etc., pinch -> `inputState.zoom`. Everything still feeds `inputState` in `src/input.js` so game code stays input-agnostic. Pointer lock does not exist on touch; never rely on it.
- **Detection**: decide touch UI with `matchMedia('(pointer: coarse)')` / `navigator.maxTouchPoints`, not the user agent. Show touch controls only on coarse pointers; a device can have both.
- **Layout/UI**: HUD and menus use relative units (`%`, `vw/vh`, `dvh`, `clamp()`) and flexbox/grid, no fixed pixel layouts. Tap targets >= 44px. Respect notches with `env(safe-area-inset-*)` (viewport already has `viewport-fit=cover`). Text readable at 360px width. No hover-only interactions. Add `touch-action: none` on the canvas and game controls so the browser does not scroll/zoom.
- **Resize/orientation**: anything sized to the screen (camera aspect, renderer, HUD) must react to `resize` (covers orientation changes). Use `visualViewport` where the on-screen keyboard or browser bars matter.
- **Performance**: mobile GPUs are weak. Respect the `graphics_quality` presets in `src/config.js` (cap DPR, shadow map size, draw distance); default to a lower preset on coarse-pointer devices. Avoid per-frame allocations; keep draw calls and texture sizes low.
- **Testing**: check every UI/input change at a phone viewport (e.g. 375x812 and landscape 812x375) with touch emulation, plus desktop, before calling it done.
